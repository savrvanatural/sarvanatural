## 1. Intent Clustering Framework

Google's Helpful Content and Quality Rater guidelines (E-E-A-T) reward authentic, verified classical formulations with transparent laboratory documentation over generic wellness affiliate content. 

Keywords are clustered into 4 primary intent silos:
1. **Navigational / Branded Intent:** Users looking specifically for SARVA NATURAL, its lineage, products, or order tracking.
2. **Transactional / Product Intent:** Shoppers looking to buy pure, vegetarian, heavy-metal tested Moringa, Triphala, or Ashwagandha capsules.
3. **Commercial Investigation / Category Intent:** Users comparing Ayurvedic formulations, 100% vegetarian HPMC capsules vs. gelatin, or looking for GMP certified classical preparations.
4. **Informational & Diagnostic Intent:** People researching Dosha imbalances, Anupana (co-drink timing), Dinacharya, and booking doctor consultations.
